# BEGIN PROB

Jumping spiders prefer humidity to be between $50$ and $70$ percent. Suppose the `"humidity"` column has mean 60, and the range $[48, 72]$ is the smallest range guaranteed to contain at least $75\%$ of the values in `"humidity"`. What is the standard deviation of `"humidity"`?

# BEGIN SOLUTION

**Answer**: $6$

According to Chebyshev's inequality, at least \(1 - \frac{1}{k^2}\) of the values lie within \(k\) standard deviations of the mean.

Since the interval [48, 72] contains at least 75% of the values, we have \(1 - \frac{1}{k^2} = 0.75\), which gives \(k = 2\).

The interval extends 12 units on either side of the mean, so 2 standard deviations equal 12. Therefore, the standard deviation is **6**.
