Ray defines the probability of events regarding a single UFO sighting:

- $C$ is the event in which a UFO is cylinder-shaped, with
  $P(C) = 0.3$.
- $D$ is the event in which a UFO is disk-shaped. $P(D)$ is unknown, but
  the event $D$ is **at least twice as likely** as the event $C$.

Recall a UFO may either be `"cylinder"`, `"sphere"`, or `"disk"`.

$S$ is the event in which a UFO is sphere-shaped. What is the maximum
possible value of $P(S)$? **Show your work.**

# BEGIN SOLUTION

**Answer:** $0.1$

Every UFO is exactly one of the three shapes, so the three events are
mutually exclusive and together cover every possibility:

$$P(C) + P(D) + P(S) = 1$$

Substituting $P(C) = 0.3$ and rearranging,

$$P(S) = 1 - 0.3 - P(D) = 0.7 - P(D)$$

To make $P(S)$ as large as possible, we want $P(D)$ as small as
possible. We're told $D$ is at least twice as likely as $C$, so

$$P(D) \geq 2 \cdot 0.3 = 0.6$$

The smallest allowed value is $P(D) = 0.6$, which gives

$$P(S) = 0.7 - 0.6 = 0.1$$

# END SOLUTION

# BEGIN SUBPROB

Marta learns the following about her rural town:

- On any given night, the probability of a UFO sighting occurring in
  town is $0.2$.
- There are 5 farms in Marta's town. On nights where a UFO is sighted,
  there is $0.3$ probability that a crop circle is created at a farm,
  independently for each farm.

What is the probability that a UFO is sighted **and** at least one crop
circle is created on any given night? You do **not** have to simplify
your answer. **Show your work.**

# BEGIN SOLUTION

**Answer:** $0.2 \cdot (1 - 0.7^5)$

We need both things to happen, so we multiply the probability of a
sighting by the probability of at least one crop circle *given* that a
sighting occurred.

The probability of a sighting is $0.2$.

For the second piece, "at least one" is easiest to handle through its
complement, which is "no crop circles at all." Each farm independently
avoids a crop circle with probability $1 - 0.3 = 0.7$, and since the
five farms are independent, the probability that **none** of them gets a
crop circle is

$$0.7^5$$

So the probability that at least one crop circle is created, on a night
when a UFO is sighted, is $1 - 0.7^5$.

Multiplying the two pieces together gives

$$0.2 \cdot (1 - 0.7^5)$$

# END SOLUTION
