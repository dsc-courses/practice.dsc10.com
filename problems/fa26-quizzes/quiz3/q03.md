# BEGIN PROB

The `"num_scares"` column of `doors` is **strongly right-skewed**, as a few unfortunate students have been scared dozens of times. Suppose we run the following code:

```py
num_scares = doors.get("num_scares")
su = (num_scares - num_scares.mean()) / np.std(num_scares)
```

Which of the following statements **must** be true? **Select all that apply.**

[ ] At least 84% of the values in `su` are between -2.5 and 2.5.
[ ] The distribution of `su` is roughly normal.
[ ] The mean and median of `su` are both 0.
[ ] `su` has a mean of 0 and a standard deviation of 1.
[ ] None of the above.

# BEGIN SOLUTION

**Answer:** Options 1 and 4

**Option 1: True.** Chebyshev's inequality says that for any distribution, at least 1 − 1/z² of the values are within z standard deviations of the mean. `su` is in standard units, so being between -2.5 and 2.5 means being within 2.5 standard deviations of the mean. 1 − 1/2.5² = 1 − 0.16 = 0.84.

**Option 2: False.** Converting to standard units shifts and rescales the data, but it doesn't change the shape. `su` is still right-skewed.

**Option 3: False.** The mean of `su` is 0. But in right-skewed data, the median is below the mean, so the median of `su` is below 0.

**Option 4: True.** Values in standard units always have a mean of 0 and a standard deviation of 1.
