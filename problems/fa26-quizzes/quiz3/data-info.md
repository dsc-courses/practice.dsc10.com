Monsters, Inc. powers its factory with the screams of DSC 10 students. Each night, monsters open closet doors into students' rooms and scare them by whispering things like "the Midterm Project is due tomorrow," "calculate the probability," and "you have used 6 out of 6 slip days."

The DataFrame `doors` has **500 rows**, one for each DSC 10 student. Its columns are:

- `"door_id"` (int): an identifier for the door belonging to this student's room.
- `"scream_units"` (float): the total amount of energy collected from this student's screams, across all scares.
- `"num_scares"` (int): the number of times the student has been scared.
