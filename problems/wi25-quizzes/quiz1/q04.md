# BEGIN SOLUTION

**Answer**: 
`500 * 0.5 ** np.arange(1,5)`
or 
`500/2 ** np.arange(1,5)`

Each meeting costs half of the previous one, so the costs are:

$$500 \cdot 0.5^1,\ 500 \cdot 0.5^2,\ 500 \cdot 0.5^3,\ 500 \cdot 0.5^4 = 250,\ 125,\ 62.5,\ 31.25$$

The exponents run from 1 to 4, which is exactly `np.arange(1, 5)`. The stop value isn't included. Arithmetic on an array is applied to every element, so `0.5 ** np.arange(1, 5)` gives `[0.5, 0.25, 0.125, 0.0625]`, and multiplying by 500 gives the four costs.

This also depends on precedence: `**` runs before `*` and `/`. So `500 * 0.5 ** ...` raises 0.5 to each power first and then multiplies, which is what we want.
