# BEGIN PROB

You want to estimate the proportion of all flower fields in the world
that use fertilizer. You will create a **68% confidence interval** for
this proportion, based on a sample of flower fields. You want your
confidence interval to have a width of **at most 0.01**. Using the fact
that the standard deviation of any dataset of 0s and 1s is no more than
0.5, calculate the **minimum sample size required**. Give your answer as
an **integer**.

# BEGIN SOLUTION

**Answer**: 10,000

**Explanation:**

By the Central Limit Theorem, the distribution of the sample proportion is roughly normal, with

$$\text{SD of sample proportion} = \frac{\text{population SD}}{\sqrt{n}}$$

A **68%** confidence interval covers about **1 SD** on either side of the sample proportion, so

$$\text{width} = 2 \cdot \frac{\text{population SD}}{\sqrt{n}}$$

The population SD of 0s and 1s is at most 0.5, so the worst case is:

$$2 \cdot \frac{0.5}{\sqrt{n}} \le 0.01 \;\Longrightarrow\; \frac{1}{\sqrt{n}} \le 0.01 \;\Longrightarrow\; \sqrt{n} \ge 100 \;\Longrightarrow\; n \ge 10000$$
