# BEGIN PROB

The course tutors are constantly competing to give the best, most thoughtful answers on Campuswire. To determine who is the nicest tutor, the course staff run a weekly **Kindness Check**: they select **4 posts at random, without replacement**, from `posts` and read every tutor reply on them. Since only questions receive replies, Jeffrey is hoping that the Kindness Check lands on more questions than notes. That way, he has more chances to show everyone just how nice he can be.

Fill in the blanks below so that `prob_more_questions` is an estimate of the probability that a Kindness Check contains **more questions than notes**.

```py
repetitions = 10000
count = 0
for i in np.arange(repetitions):
    check = np.random.choice(__(a)__)
    if __(b)__:
        count = count + 1
prob_more_questions = count / repetitions
```
