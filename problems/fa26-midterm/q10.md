# BEGIN SUBPROB

How many **private** questions took **at least 40 minutes** to receive a response? Give your answer as an **integer**.

# BEGIN SOLUTION

**Answer:** `50`

The fraction below 40 minutes is $20(0.015) + 20(0.01) = 0.5$, so $100(1 - 0.5) = 50$.
