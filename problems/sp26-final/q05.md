Bianca standardizes the `"albums"` column, which has a mean of 7 and a standard deviation of 2 before standardization.

# BEGIN SUBPROB

What are the mean and standard deviation of the `"albums"` column after standardization?

# BEGIN SOLUTION

**Answer:**

- **Mean:** 0
- **SD:** 1

**Solution:**

Standardization converts each value into standard units by subtracting the mean and dividing by the standard deviation. This transformation produces a distribution with a **mean of 0** and a **standard deviation of 1**, regardless of the original mean and standard deviation (as long as the original SD is nonzero).

The band *Joy Division* has an `"albums"` value that is $-2.5$ standard units. How many albums did this band release?

::: center
:::

# BEGIN SOLUTION

**Answer:**

2 albums

**Solution:**

To convert a standardized value back into its original units, use $x = \text{mean} + z \cdot \text{SD}$. Substituting the given values, we get $x = 7 + (-2.5)(2) = 2$. Therefore, Joy Division released **2 albums**.

What proportion of bands have an `"albums"` value in the interval `[5, 9]`? Choose the best answer below.

( ) Approximately 0.68.
( ) Approximately 0.95.
( ) At least 0.75, but we don't know exactly.
( ) At least 0.88, but we don't know exactly.
( ) We cannot determine anything about this proportion.

# BEGIN SOLUTION

**Answer:**

Option 5: We cannot determine anything about this proportion.

**Solution:**

The interval `[5, 9]` extends from one standard deviation below the mean to one standard deviation above it, since the mean is 7 and the standard deviation is 2. **Chebyshev's inequality** guarantees a proportion of at least $1-\frac{1}{k^2}$ within $k$ standard deviations, but for $k=1$, this lower bound is 0 and provides no useful information. Although approximately 68% of values fall within one standard deviation of the mean in a normal distribution, we are not told that the `"albums"` distribution is normal. Therefore, we cannot determine the proportion from the information given.

# BEGIN SUBPROB

Which of the following **could not** be the value of a band's `"albums"` in standard units? **Select all** that apply.

[ ] $-4$
[ ] $-0.5$
[ ] $0$
[ ] $1.75$
[ ] $8.5$
[ ] None of the above.

# BEGIN SOLUTION

**Answer:**

Option 1 and Option 4

**Solution:**

To convert a value in standard units back to the original number of albums, we use $x = 7 + 2z$, where $z$ is the standardized value. For **Option 1**, $z=-4$ gives $x=7+2(-4)=-1$, which is impossible because a band cannot release a negative number of albums. For **Option 4**, $z=1.75$ gives $x=7+2(1.75)=10.5$, which is impossible because the number of albums must be a whole number. The remaining values correspond to possible nonnegative integer album counts: $-0.5 \rightarrow 6$, $0 \rightarrow 7$, and $8.5 \rightarrow 24$. Therefore, only Options 1 and 4 could not be valid standardized album counts.
