# BEGIN PROB

Kate loves Studio Ghibli and she has been recording her viewings of
their films in the `kate` DataFrame, which is shown in full below. Note
that `kate` has repeated rows since Kate watched some films twice.

<center><img src="../../assets/images/sp25-quizzes/quiz2_kate.jpg" width=400></center>

In `ghibli`, there are 11 films directed by Hayao Miyazaki and only 2
directed by Hiromasa Yonebayashi.

How many rows are in the DataFrame resulting from
`ghibli.merge(kate, on="Director")`?

# BEGIN SOLUTION

**Answer:** 81

**Solution:**
For **every single row** (film) that is directed by Hayao Miyazaki in `ghibli`, it will match with **all** of the rows (films) in `kate` that have a `Director` of "Hayao Miyazaki". We know that there are `11` rows in `ghibli` that have a `Director` attribute of "Hayao Miyazaki". We know that there are `7` rows in `kate` that have have the same (`Director` attribute of "Hayao Miyazaki"). **Each** of the `11` rows in `ghibli` will match with **all** `7` of the rows in `kate`. With math, we can write this as `11 * 7 = 77`. We can now do the same with Hiromasa Yonebayashi films. There are `2` films directed by Hiromasa Yonebayashi in `ghibli`. In `kate`, there are `2` films that she has watched that are directed by Hiromasa Yonebayashi. **Each** of the `2` rows in `ghibli` will match with **all** `2` of the rows in `kate`. With math, we can write this as `2 * 2 = 4`. Now, we know the final DataFrame will have `77 + 4 = 81` rows.
