# BEGIN SOLUTION

**Solution:**

How many of these films earned a revenue more than five times their
budget?

# BEGIN SOLUTION

**Answer:** 4

**Solution:**

Let's move left to right and analyze each dot along the way. The first dot has a budget of around $19 million. Five times of this is given by the following expression: `19 * 5 = 95`. This dot has a revenue of around $275 million, which is much higher than $95 million. We can repeat this process with the rest of the dots. You will notice that the first four dots follow this pattern, but the last two do not. The second-to-last dot has a budget of $30 million. Five times of this is $150 million, but the revenue for this dot is only around $120 million. Similarly, the last dot has a budget of $100 million. Five times of this is $500 million, but the revenue for this dot is only around $170 million.
