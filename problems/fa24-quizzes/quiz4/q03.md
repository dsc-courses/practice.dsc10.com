# BEGIN PROB

The table below shows how many movies in each of four genres Jack and
Eric have seen. What is the total variation distance (TVD) between Jack
and Eric's distribution of movies by genre?

::: center
         `"Musical"`   `"Comedy"`   `"Action"`   `"Horror"`   Total
  ------ ------------- ------------ ------------ ------------ -------
  Jack   2             14           2            2            20
  Eric   55            15           15           15           100

:::

# BEGIN SOLUTION
**Answer**: 0.55

TVD compares two **distributions**, so first turn each person's counts into proportions. Jack watched 20 movies and Eric watched 100.

| | `"Musical"` | `"Comedy"` | `"Action"` | `"Horror"` |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Jack | 2/20 = 0.10 | 14/20 = 0.70 | 2/20 = 0.10 | 2/20 = 0.10 |
| Eric | 55/100 = 0.55 | 15/100 = 0.15 | 15/100 = 0.15 | 15/100 = 0.15 |
| Absolute difference | 0.45 | 0.55 | 0.05 | 0.05 |

The TVD is half the sum of the absolute differences:

$$\text{TVD} = \frac{0.45 + 0.55 + 0.05 + 0.05}{2} = \frac{1.10}{2} = 0.55$$
