# BEGIN PROB

Suppose we've imported the `scipy` module. To the nearest 0.5, what does
the following expression evaluate to?

`scipy.stats.norm.cdf(-2) * 100`

::: center
:::

# BEGIN SOLUTION
**Answer:** `2.5`

`scipy.stats.norm.cdf(z)` gives the area under the standard normal curve to the left of `z`. So `scipy.stats.norm.cdf(-2)` is the proportion of the standard normal distribution that lies more than 2 standard deviations below the mean. About 95% of values fall within ±2 standard deviations of the mean, which leaves 5% in the two tails. Since the normal curve is symmetric, each tail has 2.5%, so the left tail has a proportion of about 0.025. Multiplying by 100 gives `2.5`.
