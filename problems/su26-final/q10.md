We are interested in predicting The Parlor’s revenue based on the temperature of any given day.

- The mean and standard deviation of the `revenue` column are $c$ and $d$, respectively.
- Recall the correlation coefficient $r$ of `revenue` vs. `temp` is $\frac{3}{4}$.
- The range of temperatures in `temp` goes from $[75, 100]$. Every temperature in this range appears exactly once as an int.

# BEGIN SUBPROB

<!-- b) (X pts) -->

Use the regression line to predict the revenue on a day when the temperature is 2 standard deviations above average. **Express your answer in terms of $c$ and $d$.**

# BEGIN SOLUTION

**Answer:** $c + \frac{3d}{2}$.

Let $x$ be temperature, $y$ be revenue, and $\widehat y$ be predicted revenue. Lecture 24 relates the temperature and the prediction in standard units.

$$
\frac{\widehat y - \bar y}{\mathrm{SD}(y)}
= r \cdot \frac{x - \bar x}{\mathrm{SD}(x)}.
$$

The temperature is 2 standard deviations above its mean, so $\frac{x - \bar x}{\mathrm{SD}(x)} = 2$. Substitute $r = \frac{3}{4}$, $\bar y = c$, and $\mathrm{SD}(y) = d$.

$$
\frac{\widehat y - c}{d} = \frac{3}{4} \cdot 2 = \frac{3}{2}
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad \widehat y = c + \frac{3d}{2}.
$$

The prediction is $\frac{3}{2}$ revenue standard deviations above the mean. Multiplying by $d$ gives that increase in dollars, and adding $c$ gives the predicted revenue.

# END SOLUTION

<!-- c) (X pts) -->

The regression line predicted a revenue of $c$ for some unknown temperature $t$. What is the value of $t$ in Fahrenheit? **Give your answer as an exact decimal.**

# BEGIN SOLUTION

**Answer:** $87.5$.

In original units, Lecture 24 gives the regression line $\widehat y = mx + b$ with slope $m$ and intercept $b$.

$$
m = r \cdot \frac{\mathrm{SD}(y)}{\mathrm{SD}(x)},
\qquad b = \bar y - m\bar x.
$$

Here $x = t$, the mean revenue is $\bar y = c$, and the predicted revenue is $\widehat y = c$. Using the intercept formula gives

$$
c = mt + (c - m\bar x)
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad m(t - \bar x) = 0.
$$

The slope is positive because $r = \frac{3}{4}$ and both standard deviations are positive. Therefore $t = \bar x$, the mean temperature. Each integer from 75 through 100 occurs once. Pairing the lowest with the highest, the second lowest with the second highest, and so on gives the same midpoint for every pair.

$$
t = \bar x = \frac{75 + 100}{2} = 87.5.
$$
